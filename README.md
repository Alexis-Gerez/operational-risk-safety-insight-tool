Operational Risk & Safety Insight Tool
Overview

Operational Risk & Safety Insight Tool is a conceptual and operational support application designed to visualize, analyze, and manage operational risks in technical and industrial environments.

The tool is not intended to replace certified Health & Safety management systems. Instead, it demonstrates how operational risk concepts, continuous improvement principles, and data-driven thinking can be translated into a flexible, site-specific digital solution.

This project was developed as a professional case study, combining industrial experience, operational management criteria, and applied data visualization.

Context and Motivation

In many workshops, plants, and technical organizations, operational risk management relies on a combination of formal procedures, informal observations, and experience-based decisions.

Commercial tools often provide standardized solutions that are difficult to adapt to local processes, workflows, and training needs.

This application explores an alternative approach:

Designing a lightweight, modular system that can be adapted to different operational realities, used as a training and analysis tool, and serve as a foundation for customized risk management solutions.

Purpose of the Tool

The main objectives of this project are:

Support structured observation of operational risks

Visualize risk priority and improvement opportunities

Connect risk observations with corrective and preventive actions

Reinforce continuous improvement (Kaizen) thinking

Serve as a training and simulation tool for supervisors and technical teams

The tool emphasizes understanding processes and risks rather than generating formal compliance records.

Functional Modules

Dashboard
Provides a global operational overview, showing key indicators such as total risk observations, high-priority cases, open actions, and training-related situations.

Risk Observation
Allows structured registration of operational risk observations, including context, severity, probability, priority level, and training relevance.

Priority Matrix
Visualizes risks based on severity and probability, helping identify critical zones and prioritize actions using a clear, intuitive matrix.

Improvement Actions
Links risks to improvement actions, enabling follow-up of corrective and preventive measures and reinforcing accountability.

Training View
Highlights risks with training relevance, supporting technical education, awareness sessions, and continuous learning initiatives.

Target Use Cases

Automotive workshops and service centers

Industrial maintenance environments

Technical training institutions

Supervisory and middle-management training

Continuous improvement and operational excellence programs

This tool is especially suited for environments where practical experience, process knowledge, and adaptability are valued.
